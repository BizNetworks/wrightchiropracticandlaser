Good to Know

Tips for a Smoother Recovery

Small habits can make sports injury rehabilitation more effective between visits.

Don't push through sharp pain

Soreness is normal; sharp or worsening pain is a signal to pause and get checked.

Keep gently moving

Light, comfortable movement often helps healing more than complete rest. Ask us what suits your injury.

Rebuild gradually

Returning too fast is a common cause of re-injury. We help you progress in sensible stages.

Who is sports injury rehabilitation for?

Anyone dealing with an activity-related injury, from competitive athletes to people hurt while gardening or lifting at home.

Is laser therapy painful?

Most people describe it as painless, often with mild warmth. Your provider will explain what to expect during your first session.

How many visits will I need?

It depends on the injury, how long you've had it and your goals. We'll outline a realistic plan after your assessment.
